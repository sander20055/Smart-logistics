How to read the traffic light

Based on the rubric in the Excel. SA = subassignment.

  • On track
    Handed in, quality good or sufficient
  • Quality insufficient
    Handed in, quality insufficient
  • Not handed in
    Nothing handed in, whatever the quality column says
  • Awaiting grade
    Handed in, not graded yet (not counted)
  • Board attention
    Not handed in 2 or more times. Escalated to the CEOs: shown at the top and marked in the matrix.
  • Presented
    One icon per presentation. Not part of the traffic light.

Trend compares the last two graded SAs (on track > quality insufficient > not handed in).
