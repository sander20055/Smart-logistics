Understanding of the project

FreshWay is the fictional supermarket logistics case in Windesheim Smart Logistics. The final product is an integrated company vision; your department is Outbound transport. SA3 proposed window-aware planning and live monitoring. The actual submitted SA4 added arrival-risk and unloading-time prediction with planner approval. SA5 extends this into bounded coordination on a shared company AI platform.

What changed during review

  • The actual submitted SA4 was checked in addition to partial repository contributions.
  • C03/C10 are now available. Store access, dock, noise and subcontractor restrictions still require route/store-specific validation.
  • Peer review does not automatically equal Tier 1 production evidence.
  • 19,242 valid deliveries remain after excluding 488 appended analysis rows. Fifty-eight timestamp/flag discrepancies need measurement reconciliation.
  • Arrival punctuality is separate from OTIF. Missing required/accepted quantities prevent full OTIF calculation.
  • Guaranteed cost savings, planner45→10minutes, binding95% ZE and autonomous charging claims were removed from the assembled design.
